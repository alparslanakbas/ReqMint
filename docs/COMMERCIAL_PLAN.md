# ReqMint commercial plan

ReqMint earns trust before it asks for payment. The product remains local-first, the website has no checkout or account system, and purchases are handled by trusted platform stores.

## Launch model

### Public preview

- Price: **free** on every supported platform.
- No card, account, forced telemetry, or artificial request limit.
- Goal: validate installation, onboarding, stability, retention, and which advanced workflows create measurable value.
- Minimum observation window: one stable public-preview cycle before any paid feature launches.

### Community

- Price: **free forever**.
- Includes the complete daily API request loop, local workspaces, collections, environments, local history, core assertions and collection runs, Git-friendly files, themes, English/Turkish localization, and guided onboarding.
- Privacy, accessibility, security fixes, workspace portability, and basic Git review are never treated as luxury features.

### Pro

- Target list price: **USD 39.99 per year**, regionally priced by the platform store.
- Target trial: **14 days**, managed by the store where supported.
- Candidate value: advanced data-driven runs and reports, deeper search/history tools, productivity automation, professional import/migration tools, and future power-user workflows.
- Pro is not published until the paid boundary is implemented, tested, documented, and demonstrably valuable.

The list price is not launched at a permanently discounted amount. A free preview and time-limited trial reduce adoption risk without anchoring the product at an unsustainable price. Lifetime deals and permanent “founder” price locks are excluded from the initial model because ongoing desktop updates, signing, support, and platform validation create recurring costs.

### Team

- Target list price: **USD 6 per user/month when billed annually**.
- Deferred until ReqMint has real team administration, policy, audit, support, and portable entitlement capabilities.
- Git-friendly Community workspaces remain usable by teams; Team pricing applies to additional organization value rather than access to users' own files.

## Store and website responsibilities

The ReqMint website:

- presents the product and plan comparison;
- links to the official Microsoft Store and future approved platform channels;
- hosts task-focused documentation, privacy, security, and release information;
- does not collect payment details or create ReqMint accounts.

The platform store owns checkout, taxes, regional pricing, receipts, subscription or add-on entitlement, refunds, signing, hosting, and updates where its channel supports them. Windows commerce starts with Microsoft Store. Community remains consistent on macOS and Linux while a trustworthy cross-platform Pro entitlement is evaluated; ReqMint will not imply that a Windows purchase is portable until it really is.

## Revenue sequence

1. Ship a trustworthy free public preview and earn reviews, GitHub stars, documentation traffic, and Store discovery.
2. Use Store analytics, release downloads, support questions, and voluntary feedback—without forced in-app telemetry—to validate activation and demand.
3. Define Pro only around repeatedly requested, high-value workflows.
4. Publish Pro at its real list price with a trial instead of a permanently cheap launch price.
5. Add Team only after organizations request administration and support that justify per-seat pricing.

## Price benchmark recorded 24 August 2026

At the time of this decision, annually billed competitor entry prices were approximately USD 6/user/month for Bruno Pro and Hoppscotch Organization, USD 9/month for Postman Solo, USD 12/user/month for Insomnia Pro, and USD 19/user/month for Postman Team. ReqMint Pro's target sits below established individual products while preserving room to fund maintenance; Team enters at the lower end only after it has authentic team value.

Every public price must be rechecked against current store rules, taxes, exchange rates, competitor plans, and the implemented feature set before submission.
